# Working title: (unnamed) Orange County zombie survival

Single-player first-person survival in a seeded, fictional Orange County, California. Our own simple 3D style.

## Decisions (user, 2026-10-13)
- Smooth low-poly 3D (not voxel). First person only, no third person.
- Setting: a sunny, pastel, bleached-out Southern California, fictionalised (no real brands or trademarks; real geography only).
- Survival basics: **hydration and food**. **No heat/cold system.**
- Otherwise follow Unturned's system list, in our own style. Mechanics are fair to copy; its assets, names and branding are not.
- Our style: simple shape language, flat colour, a fixed palette from the atomic-age scheme, one rigged base body with swappable outfits.
- Single-player first; keep the structure ready for co-op later.

## Systems (from Unturned)
Core
- Vitals: health, food, water, stamina, (infection); bleeding, broken legs.
- Inventory: grid, with storage from clothing; hotbar; item condition (wears down).
- Loot: spawn points with tables per place type; respawn.
- Crafting and cooking: recipes; food and drink variety.
- Skills and XP: three branches (offense, defense, support). Built after the core loop.
Threats
- Zombies: several types, notice by sight and sound; hordes.
- Animals: huntable.
- Combat: guns (recoil, range falloff, attachments), melee, headshots, throwables.
World and building
- Seeded world: districts (suburb, strip mall, business park, beach, harbor, freeway, hills).
- Base building: walls, floors, doors, storage, beds, claim flags, traps.
- Resources: wood, scrap, farming, fishing.
- Weather and day/night (sun, wind, brush fire as a hazard; not temperature).
Later
- Vehicles (fuel, battery, tires), multiplayer, NPCs and quests, modding.

## Milestones
1. Walkable seeded neighbourhood in the style: cul-de-sac, houses, cars, palms, a strip mall; first-person controller; sun and shadow.
2. Vitals, inventory, loot tables, eating and drinking.
3. Zombies (one type), melee and one gun.
4. Crafting and cooking; more zombie types.
5. Base building; skills; weather; more districts.
6. Vehicles; then multiplayer.
